About Brian

Brian has been on the water in the Keys longer than most of the channel markers.

A Keys original

Looks like a beach bum. Runs a tight yard.

Brian showed up in the Keys back in the golden days, when the bay was quieter and the paperwork was lighter. He's flown a lot of things in and out of these islands since then. Mostly fishermen.

These days he runs the yard from a folding chair by the service bay, in a tank top and wraparound shades, with a cold can in hand. He knows every boat on the stack, every captain on the bay and every way a two-stroke can let you down.

His wife Lori runs the office, the books and the Friday sangria on the back deck. If you're nice, you get a glass. If you're late on rack fees, you still get a glass, but it's a smaller glass.

"Fixing boats in the Keys since before you were born."
