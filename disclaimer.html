1. Parody & fan work

Brian's Boat Works & Marina is a fictional business that appears in the video game Grand Theft Auto VI. This site imagines, for fun, what that business's own small-town website might look like. It is a creative, non-commercial fan project and a work of parody.

This is not an official Rockstar Games website, not official game content, and not a source of official information about the game.

3. Everything here is fictional

  • The business is not real. There is no Brian's Boat Works & Marina you can visit, call, hire or pay.
  • No services are offered. Nothing on this site is an offer of boat repairs, equipment, storage, rack rental or any other service.
  • Prices, hours, rates and fuel info are made up. They're part of the joke.
  • The reviews are parody. They were written for this site and are not from real customers.
  • The phone number (786-555-0109) is fictional. It appears in the game and is shown here only as part of the sign graphic. Please don't call it.
  • Places are fictional. Key Lento, the Leonida Keys, Mariana County and the state of Leonida exist only in the game. The map is an illustration, not directions.
  • The contact form sends nothing. It's a demo. No message is delivered and nobody will reply.

4. Real places, businesses & people

The game's setting is loosely inspired by the Florida Keys. This site has no connection to any real marina, boat yard or business in the Florida Keys or anywhere else, and it doesn't describe one. Any resemblance to a real business, its staff or its customers is coincidental.

The people named on this site (Brian, Lori, Jason, Cal and Donnie) are fictional characters from the game, not real people. Their names and relationships come from the game, but the job titles, descriptions and jokes about them on this site were made up for this parody and are not official game content.

5. Content & humor

The humor on this site is satire about a fictional world. Nothing here encourages, endorses or gives information about any real illegal activity.

Details from the game may change with future updates. This site isn't kept in sync with the game and may be inaccurate. It's provided "as is", for entertainment only, with no warranties of any kind.

6. Privacy

This is a static website. It has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party scripts. Fonts and icons are loaded from the site itself. Anything typed into the contact form stays in your browser and is never sent or stored. The host (such as GitHub Pages) may keep its own standard server logs under its own privacy policy.
