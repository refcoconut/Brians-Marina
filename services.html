Straight off the sign

Boat repairs, equipment & storage

Four services. Same as the sign out front. We figured if it was good enough for the building, it's good enough for the website.

01
Service bay

Boat Repairs

Outboards are our bread and butter. Hulls, electrical and the weird stuff too. Drop it off, we'll call when it's done. Or we won't, and you'll come check. Either way.

  • Outboard service, tune-ups and repowers
  • Lower units, props and steering
  • Fiberglass and gelcoat repair
  • Wiring, pumps and electronics
  • Bottom paint and wash-downs
02
Parts counter

Equipment

A small shop with a lot of shelves. If we don't have it, we know a guy, and the guy usually has it by Thursday.

  • Props, pumps and impellers
  • Dock lines, fenders and anchors
  • Safety gear and flares
  • Bottom paint and cleaners
  • Ice, bait and cold drinks
03
Dry storage

Storage

Keep your boat out of the water and out of the weather. Stored indoors or on the racks, rinsed after every trip, and strapped down when the storms show up.

  • Indoor and outdoor dry storage
  • Freshwater rinse after each launch
  • Trailer parking on site
  • Hurricane haul-out plan
  • Short-term and seasonal
04
Dry-stack

Rent a Rack

Your own slot on the stack. Call or text ahead, the forklift drops your boat in the water, and it's waiting at the dock when you show up. Bring it back, we wash it and put it away.

  • Boats up to 36 ft
  • Launch on request, 1 hr notice
  • Monthly or annual terms
  • Wash-down included
  • Fuel and ice on the way out

Good questions

FAQ

And a couple of bad ones we get anyway.

How much notice do you need for a launch?

About an hour. Earlier on weekends. If you call at 4:45 you're going fishing tomorrow.

Do you work on inboards?

Some. Outboards are what we do best. Bring it by and we'll tell you honestly.

What happens during hurricane season?

Rack boats get strapped, outdoor boats get moved, and everybody gets a phone call. Then we all hope.

Can I land a seaplane at your dock?

Only if you're Brian.

Do you ask a lot of questions?

No.

Need a rack before the season fills up?

Spots go fast once the snowbirds show up. Get on the list.
