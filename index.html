Since way back

A boat yard. Not a yacht club.

Green tin, wooden docks, a forklift that's older than some of our customers. Brian's has been on the bay side of Key Lento long enough that the pelicans have assigned seating.

We don't do valet, we don't do champagne, and we don't do brochures. We fix boats, we store boats, and we sell you the stuff that keeps them running.

  • Dry-stack racks up to 36 ft
  • Outboard service bay, in-house
  • Fuel, ice and bait on the dock
  • Seaplane-friendly. Long story.
